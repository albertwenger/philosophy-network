# Nāgārjuna

Nāgārjuna (conventionally c. 150–c. 250 CE; dates uncertain) was a Buddhist philosopher from the Indian subcontinent associated with the Madhyamaka school. His works examine dependence, emptiness, and the limits of claims about intrinsic nature.

[More on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagarjuna)

Biographical details and the attribution of several works are disputed. The proposals here concern the Mūlamadhyamakakārikā.
