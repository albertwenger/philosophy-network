# Robin Wall Kimmerer

Robin Wall Kimmerer (born 1953) is a botanist, writer, and enrolled member of the Citizen Potawatomi Nation. Her work brings ecological science into conversation with Indigenous knowledge, emphasizing gratitude and responsibility toward other living beings.
