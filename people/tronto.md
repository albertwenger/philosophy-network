# Joan Tronto

Joan Tronto (born 1952) is an American political theorist whose work connects feminist care ethics with democracy. She has taught at Hunter College, the City University of New York Graduate Center, and the University of Minnesota.

[More on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Tronto)
