# Avicenna

Avicenna, or Ibn Sīnā (c. 980–1037), was a philosopher and physician from the eastern Islamic world who worked in Central Asia and Iran. His Arabic and Persian writings developed influential accounts of logic, the soul, medicine, and metaphysics.

[More on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avicenna)
