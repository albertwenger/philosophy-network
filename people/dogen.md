# Dōgen

Dōgen (1200–1253) was a Japanese Zen Buddhist teacher and writer associated with the establishment of Sōtō Zen in Japan. His Shōbōgenzō explores practice, realization, language, and time through sermons and essays.

[More on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C5%8Dgen)
