# Kwame Gyekye

Kwame Gyekye (1939–2019) was a Ghanaian philosopher who taught at the University of Ghana. His work examines Akan philosophical traditions and the relationship between individual persons and communal life.

[More on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwame_Gyekye)

Biographical context: [University of Ghana memorial account](https://old1.ug.edu.gh/cfas/news/late-emeritus-prof-kwame-gyekyes-intellectual-legacy-celebrated-maiden-memorial-lecture).
