# Mencius

Mencius (approximately 371–289 BCE) was a Confucian philosopher from the state of Zou in Warring States China. The work bearing his name develops accounts of moral cultivation, humane government, and the tendency of human nature toward goodness.

[More on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mencius)
