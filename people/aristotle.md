# Aristotle

Aristotle (384–322 BCE) was a Greek philosopher who studied with [Plato](plato.md) and founded the Lyceum in Athens. His work spans logic, nature, politics, and ethics, including accounts of virtue, practical wisdom, and human flourishing.
