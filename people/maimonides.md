# Maimonides

Moses Maimonides (1135 or 1138–1204; birth year disputed) was a Jewish philosopher, legal scholar, and physician born in Córdoba who later lived in Egypt. He brought philosophical inquiry into sustained conversation with Jewish law and scriptural interpretation.
