# Next work

1. Expand the concepts and their senses, starting with gaps and connections
   around the existing entries. Ground each sense in a cited passage and link
   it to a proposal that explains the passage.
2. Add contributors and sources, broadening the range of periods, traditions,
   and perspectives. Give each contributor a brief biography and a Wikipedia
   link, and specify the source edition and passage for each interpretation.
3. Keep related meanings easy to compare while preserving real differences.
   Reuse vocabulary where the meaning is the same and make important limits
   explicit.
4. Review entries as the network grows, checking source fidelity, attribution,
   and links. Use the [source review template](templates/REVIEW.md) when seeking
   independent review.

The current selection favors familiar passages and leaves substantial gaps,
including contemporary work, medieval Islamic and Jewish philosophy, several
Indian schools, and Indigenous and oral traditions. Individual contributors
cannot represent an entire tradition.

Reader testing is deferred. It can be designed around specific questions once
the network is broader and the interpretations have received source review.
