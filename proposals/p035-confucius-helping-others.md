# Helping others to develop

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Let the wish to establish and develop yourself guide you in helping others do the same.

## Context and limits

The saying follows a question about benefiting everyone: [Confucius](../people/confucius.md) treats that as a demanding achievement even for exemplary rulers. The practical comparison with oneself is an approach to virtue, not a promise that everyone’s aspirations are identical or that benefits must be exchanged.

## Source excerpt

> 'Now the man of perfect virtue, wishing to be established himself, seeks also to establish others; wishing to be enlarged himself, he seeks also to enlarge others. 3. 'To be able to judge of others by what is nigh in ourselves;— this may be called the art of virtue.'

James Legge translation, Book VI, chapter XXVIII.2–3; initial paragraph follows the preceding reply.
