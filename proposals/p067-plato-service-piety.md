# What would service to the gods accomplish?

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Calling piety service to the gods leaves its purpose to be explained.

## Context and limits

Euthyphro calls piety the part of justice concerned with the gods. Socrates asks whether this care improves them and, when that is denied, what their service accomplishes. The dialogue returns to divine approval without securing a definition. This is an examination of Euthyphro’s proposal, not Plato’s endorsement of it.

## Source excerpt

> what is this attention to the gods which is called piety?

Benjamin Jowett translation; Socrates speaking, 13d.
