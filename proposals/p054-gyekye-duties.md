# Shared humanity creates duties

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Shared humanity grounds duties to help others.

## Context and limits

Concern for communal welfare extends beyond contractual exchanges; it does not eliminate individual standing.

## Source and attribution

[Kwame Gyekye](../people/gyekye.md); [Person and Community in African Thought](../sources/gyekye-person-community.md).

Passage: Chapter V, pp. 117–119, duties and assistance to others in distress.
