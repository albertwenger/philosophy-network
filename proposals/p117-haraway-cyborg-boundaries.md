# The cyborg unsettles natural boundaries

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

The cyborg challenges stable divisions between organism and machine, and between nature and culture.

## Context and limits

Haraway’s political figure does not imply that blurring these boundaries is automatically emancipatory.

[Donna Haraway](../people/haraway.md); [A Cyborg Manifesto](../sources/haraway-cyborg-manifesto.md).
