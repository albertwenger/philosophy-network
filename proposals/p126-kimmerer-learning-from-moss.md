# Learning persistence from moss communities

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Moss communities suggest ways of sustaining life through ecological limits and resource cycling.

## Context and limits

Kimmerer draws lessons from their energy use and recycling. The comparison invites human restraint; it does not treat a moss community as a complete plan for society.
