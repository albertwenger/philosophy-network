# The wax through its alterations

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Judging wax to remain the same body goes beyond its changing sensory appearance.

## Context and limits

Heating changes the wax’s smell, shape, and texture. The meditator understands it as extended and capable of further changes beyond those imagined. The argument concerns how a body is recognized; it does not claim that sensing is absent from the encounter.

## Source excerpt

> it is my mind alone which perceives it

Haldane and Ross translation; Meditation II, p. 154. Footnote marker omitted.
