# Reason can be situated without becoming mere power

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Studying power requires distinguishing prejudice from good reasons.

## Context and limits

Fricker opposes reducing reason entirely to power: that reduction would obscure the very contrast needed to criticize prejudicial disbelief.

## Source and attribution

[Miranda Fricker](../people/fricker.md); [Epistemic Injustice: Power and the Ethics of Knowing](../sources/fricker-epistemic-injustice.md).

Passage: Introduction, pp. 2–3, critique of reducing reason to power.
