# Gratitude can change how much we take

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Experiencing what we receive as enough can restrain the desire to take more.

## Context and limits

Gratitude supports taking only what is needed. Kimmerer still acknowledges real scarcity.
