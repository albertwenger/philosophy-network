# One moral standard across sexes

## Core

Moral virtue has the same nature for women and men.

## Qualification

The passage rejects different kinds of virtue while still conceding differences in degree between sexes. A fully egalitarian reconstruction must disclose that departure.

## Source and attribution

[Mary Wollstonecraft](../people/wollstonecraft.md); [A Vindication of the Rights of Woman](../sources/wollstonecraft-vindication.md).

Passage: Chapter II, paragraph affirming one eternal standard and its immediate preceding qualification.

This is an editorial reconstruction for evaluation. It is not a quotation or a verified statement of the author’s complete position.
