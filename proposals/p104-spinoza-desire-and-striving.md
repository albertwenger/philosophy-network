# Desire as directed striving

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Desire is our striving as directed toward some particular activity.

## Context and limits

This sense includes appetite, impulse, and willing, rather than only a conscious wish for something absent. Desires vary with our condition and may pull us in opposed directions.

## Source excerpt

> I. Desire is the actual essence of man, in so far as it is conceived, as determined to a particular activity by some given modification of itself.
>
> […]
>
> By the term desire, then, I here mean all man's endeavours, impulses, appetites, and volitions, which vary according to each man's disposition, and are, therefore, not seldom opposed one to another, according as a man is drawn in different directions, and knows not where to turn.

R. H. M. Elwes translation. Part III, Definitions of the Emotions I and explanation.
