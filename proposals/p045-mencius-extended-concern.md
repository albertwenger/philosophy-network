# Extending the concern already present

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Concern shown nearby can be extended into practical protection of people farther away.

## Context and limits

Addressing King Xuan, [Mencius](../people/mencius.md) connects compassion for an ox with the neglected needs of the people. The king’s problem is failure to extend an existing capacity, rather than sheer inability. Family affection is a starting point for wider care, not a claim that every relationship has the same form.

## Source excerpt

> Treat with the reverence due to age the elders in your own family, so that the elders in the families of others shall be similarly treated; treat with the kindness due to youth the young in your own family, so that the young in the families of others shall be similarly treated - do this, and the kingdom may be made to go round in your palm.

James Legge translation, 1A7; romanization in the commentary follows modern usage.
