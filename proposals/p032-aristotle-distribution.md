# Justice in distributing shared goods

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Just distribution matches shares to the relevant standing of those receiving them.

## Context and limits

The equality is a proportion between recipients and shares, not necessarily identical amounts. [Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md) records competing standards of merit, including freedom, wealth, and excellence; the formula alone does not settle which standard is justified. The discussion concerns people sharing in a political constitution, not a modern universal entitlement.

## Source excerpt

> Further, this is plain from the fact that awards should be 'according to merit'; for all men agree that what is just in distribution must be according to merit in some sense, though they do not all specify the same sort of merit, but democrats identify it with the status of freeman, supporters of oligarchy with wealth (or with noble birth), and supporters of aristocracy with excellence.

W. D. Ross translation, V.3, 1131a24–29.
