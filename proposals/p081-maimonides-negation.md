# Knowing through what cannot be said of God

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Negative descriptions guide understanding without revealing the divine essence.

## Context and limits

Maimonides explains how exclusions can narrow an inquiry. Applied to God, terms such as living or powerful deny death or weakness rather than add qualities. Knowing that God exists remains distinct from comprehending what God is.

[Maimonides](../people/maimonides.md); [Guide for the Perplexed](../sources/maimonides-guide.md).
