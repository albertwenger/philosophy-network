# Democracy as shared and open association

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Democratic life widens shared interests and keeps groups open to learning from one another.

## Context and limits

The test concerns social relations as well as governmental arrangements. A tightly unified group can still be undemocratic if its interests exclude others. Shared experience must cross class, racial, and national barriers; sustaining these conditions requires education and opportunities for individual initiative.

## Source excerpt

> A society which makes provision for participation in its good of all its members on equal terms and which secures flexible readjustment of its institutions through interaction of the different forms of associated life is in so far democratic.

English text, Chapter VII, summary.
