# An active middle between sense and reason

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

An aesthetic state can free the mind by bringing sensibility and reason into activity together.

## Context and limits

The middle is neither empty indecision nor an average between rival claims. Each capacity checks the other’s exclusive determination while both remain active; this prepares the possibility of free activity without itself prescribing a particular action.

## Source excerpt

> Thus, to pass from sensation to thought, the soul traverses a medium position, in which sensibility and reason are at the same time active, and thus they mutually destroy their determinant power, and by their antagonism produce a negation. This medium situation in which the soul is neither physically nor morally constrained, and yet is in both ways active, merits essentially the name of a free situation; and if we call the state of sensuous determination physical, and the state of rational determination logical or moral, that state of real and active determination should be called the aesthetic.

English translation in Project Gutenberg 6798 (translator not identified in its catalogue). Letter XX, concluding paragraphs on the medium position and aesthetic state.
