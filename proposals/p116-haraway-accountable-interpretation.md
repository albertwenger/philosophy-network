# Interpretation needs an accountable position

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Knowledge develops through critical interpretation that acknowledges its position and responsibilities.

## Context and limits

Haraway seeks better accounts through situated exchange. Neither a view from nowhere nor treating every view as interchangeable provides the accountability she proposes.
