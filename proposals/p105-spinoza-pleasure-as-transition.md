# Pleasure as a change in power

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Pleasure is a transition toward greater power of acting, rather than the possession of that power alone.

## Context and limits

“Perfection” here concerns reality or power, so the definition does not claim that every pleasant transition proves moral excellence. A change in capacity is distinguished from already possessing that capacity.

## Source excerpt

> II. Pleasure is the transition of a man from a less to a greater perfection.
>
> III. Pain is the transition of a man from a greater to a less perfection.
>
> Explanation—I say transition: for pleasure is not perfection itself. For, if man were born with the perfection to which he passes, he would possess the same, without the emotion of pleasure.

R. H. M. Elwes translation. Part III, Definitions of the Emotions II–III and explanation; compare proposition XI, note.
