# What the thinking subject does

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Doubting, willing, imagining, and seeming to sense count as present thinking.

## Context and limits

Even if an apparent object is unreal, the meditator’s present seeming to see or feel is an occurrence of thought. This supports certainty of present existence without settling every claim about the self’s enduring nature.

## Source excerpt

> A thing which thinks.

Haldane and Ross translation; Meditation II, p. 152.
