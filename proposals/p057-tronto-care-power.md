# Power can run toward the recipient

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

A recipient can command care rather than depend on a more powerful caregiver.

## Context and limits

[Tronto](../people/tronto.md) uses Waerness’s distinction between necessary care and personal service to expose differing power relations.

## Source and attribution

[Joan Tronto](../people/tronto.md); [Caring Democracy](../sources/tronto-caring-democracy.md).

Passage: Chapter 1, “The Meaning of Care and Caring”, paragraphs on Waerness’s categories and who commands care work.
