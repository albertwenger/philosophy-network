# Reading modernity through colonial relations

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

A history centered within Europe can conceal the colonial relations that helped constitute modernity.

## Context and limits

Dussel proposes a world-historical interpretation beginning with the expansion of 1492. This is his contested historical thesis, not a neutral chronology.
