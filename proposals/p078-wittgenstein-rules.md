# Understanding a rule in practice

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Understanding rules appears in their application.

## Context and limits

Further interpretations cannot by themselves secure correct application. Section 202 distinguishes following a rule from merely believing oneself to follow it.

## Source and attribution

[Ludwig Wittgenstein](../people/wittgenstein.md); [Philosophische Untersuchungen](../sources/wittgenstein-investigations.md).

Passage: Part I, §§198–202: interpretation, custom, and rule-following.
