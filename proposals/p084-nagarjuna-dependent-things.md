# Dependence and emptiness

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Conditional arising and emptiness belong together.

## Context and limits

The verses connect dependence with absence of an independent nature. Emptiness preserves conditional functioning; it is not a claim that nothing matters or happens.

[Nāgārjuna](../people/nagarjuna.md); [Mūlamadhyamakakārikā](../sources/nagarjuna-middle-verses.md).
