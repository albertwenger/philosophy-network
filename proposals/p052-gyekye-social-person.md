# Persons exceed their social formation

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Community nurtures persons without wholly creating their capacities.

## Context and limits

[Gyekye](../people/gyekye.md) resists reducing personhood to social approval while acknowledging natural sociality.

## Source and attribution

[Kwame Gyekye](../people/gyekye.md); [Person and Community in African Thought](../sources/gyekye-person-community.md).

Passage: Chapter V, pp. 112–114, concluding discussion before “Rights, Duties and the Communal Structure”.
