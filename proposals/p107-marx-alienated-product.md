# When a product gains power over its maker

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Alienation occurs when a worker’s product becomes a power over its producer.

## Context and limits

Marx locates this estrangement within private-property relations; producing an external object is not itself the problem.

## Source and attribution

[Karl Marx](../people/marx.md); [Economic and Philosophic Manuscripts of 1844](../sources/marx-1844-manuscripts.md).

Passage: First Manuscript, Estranged Labour, manuscript page XXII, discussion of the product as an alien object.
