# Reason can direct a motive without supplying it

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Reason discovers relations and means but does not motivate action by itself.

## Context and limits

Hume distinguishes discovering causes from caring about their effects. Reason can correct a false assumption or ineffective means, while a desire supplies the impulse. His restriction on reason does not make every desire morally acceptable.

## Source excerpt

> reason alone can never be a motive to any action of the will

David Hume; II.III.III, statement of the first claim to be argued.
