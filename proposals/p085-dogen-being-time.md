# Existing and time

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Existing things are inseparable from time.

## Context and limits

Time appears through concrete beings and activity; it cannot be understood merely as departure.

## Source and attribution

[Dōgen](../people/dogen.md); [Shōbōgenzō](../sources/dogen-shobogenzo.md).

Passage: Book 1, Uji (chapter 11), [29]–[32], printed pp. 91–93; PDF pp. 108–110.
