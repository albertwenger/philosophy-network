# Care must answer to democratic commitments

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Democratic care must accord with justice, equality, and freedom.

## Context and limits

The fifth phase adds political standards; calling something care does not make it good.

## Source and attribution

[Joan Tronto](../people/tronto.md); [Caring Democracy](../sources/tronto-caring-democracy.md).

Passage: Chapter 1, “The Meaning of Care and Caring”, fifth phase and concluding discussion of bad care.
