# Virtue and the mean

Draft interpretation of the cited passage; not a quotation.

## Short version

Moral virtue avoids excess and deficiency through appropriate choice.

## Context and limits

This concerns settled character. The mean depends on the person and circumstances. Reason determines it, as a person of practical wisdom would determine it. It is not a numerical midpoint, and some actions admit no virtuous mean.

[Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md); [Nicomachean Ethics, Book II](../sources/aristotle-ethics-ii.md).
