# Virtue and the mean

## Core

Moral virtue avoids excess and deficiency through appropriate choice.

## Qualification

This concerns settled character, judgment, and circumstances. The mean is not arithmetic, and some actions admit no virtuous mean.

## Source and attribution

[Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md); [Nicomachean Ethics, Book II](../sources/aristotle-ethics-ii.md).
