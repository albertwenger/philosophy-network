# Estrangement within the act of working

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Work itself can become alienating when the activity belongs to another.

## Context and limits

The worker experiences production as compelled rather than as an expression of their capacities.

## Source and attribution

[Karl Marx](../people/marx.md); [Economic and Philosophic Manuscripts of 1844](../sources/marx-1844-manuscripts.md).

Passage: First Manuscript, Estranged Labour, manuscript page XXIII, discussion beginning with what constitutes alienation of labor.
