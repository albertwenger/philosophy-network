# Ideas and their experiential materials

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Ideas are fainter perceptions built from experiential materials.

## Context and limits

Hume contrasts lively impressions with less forceful ideas and describes imagination as recombining materials. His later missing-shade example qualifies the apparent universality of the copy claim.

## Source and attribution

[David Hume](../people/hume.md); [An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding](../sources/hume-enquiry.md).

Passage: Section II, numbered paragraphs 11–17, including the missing shade of blue.
