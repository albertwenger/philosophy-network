# Dignity supports rights within community

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Communal membership cannot erase personal dignity or the claims it supports.

## Context and limits

[Gyekye](../people/gyekye.md) defends individual rights without treating every right as absolute.

## Source and attribution

[Kwame Gyekye](../people/gyekye.md); [Person and Community in African Thought](../sources/gyekye-person-community.md).

Passage: Chapter V, pp. 114–117, “Rights, Duties and the Communal Structure”.
