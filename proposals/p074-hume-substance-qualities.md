# Substance and its supposed bearer

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

We conceive collections of qualities, not a separately perceived bearer beneath them.

## Context and limits

Hume asks which impression could supply an idea of substance beyond qualities. He finds none and explains how imagination unites qualities under a name. The target is the claimed idea of an additional support, not the existence of ordinary named objects.

## Source and attribution

[David Hume](../people/hume.md); [A Treatise of Human Nature](../sources/hume-treatise.md).

Passage: Book I, part I, section VI, opening three paragraphs on modes and substances.
