# Educational aims belong within learning

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

A good educational aim guides present activity while preserving the learner’s capacity to grow.

## Context and limits

Aims belong to particular people and situations, and should be revised as experience develops. They are not fixed external destinations to which learners are merely instruments. [Dewey](../people/dewey.md) connects continuing growth for all with democratic relations: unequal social power can make some people’s nominal aims serve others’ purposes.

## Source excerpt

> And it is well to remind ourselves that education as such has no aims. Only persons, parents, and teachers, etc., have aims, not an abstract idea like education. And consequently their purposes are indefinitely varied, differing with different children, changing as children grow and with the growth of experience on the part of the one who teaches.

English text, Chapter VIII, section 3, “Applications in Education”.
