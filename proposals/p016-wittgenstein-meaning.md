# Meaning through use

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

For many cases, a word’s meaning can be explained by its use.

## Context and limits

The passage explicitly limits its scope; it does not claim that use explains all meaning.

## Source and attribution

[Ludwig Wittgenstein](../people/wittgenstein.md); [Philosophische Untersuchungen](../sources/wittgenstein-investigations.md).

Passage: Section 43, including its opening restriction and final sentence.
