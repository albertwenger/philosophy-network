# Learning to express pain

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Pain words can replace natural expressions of pain.

## Context and limits

The child learns an expression, not a report about crying. The discussion distinguishes having pain from learning about it; it does not deny sensations.

## Source and attribution

[Ludwig Wittgenstein](../people/wittgenstein.md); [Philosophische Untersuchungen](../sources/wittgenstein-investigations.md).

Passage: Part I, §§244–246: learning pain words and the language of knowing pain.
