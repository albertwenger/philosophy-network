# How intellectual understanding develops

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Intellectual capacity develops through principles, reasoning, and comparison of sensory materials.

## Context and limits

Avicenna distinguishes basic axioms from conclusions earned through demonstration. He also describes comparing sensory forms to separate shared and differing features. This belongs to his account of the rational soul and does not reduce every intellectual principle to sensation.

## Source and attribution

[Avicenna](../people/avicenna.md); [A Compendium on the Soul](../sources/avicenna-compendium.md).
