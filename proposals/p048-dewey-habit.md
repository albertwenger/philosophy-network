# Habits can keep learning alive

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Habits support growth when they remain intelligent capacities for adapting action.

## Context and limits

Habit is an acquired ability to use an environment, not simply mechanical repetition. Learning from earlier consequences can equip us for new situations. [Dewey](../people/dewey.md) also recognizes routine habits that arrest adaptability; therefore forming a habit does not by itself make education good.

## Source excerpt

> Active habits involve thought, invention, and initiative in applying capacities to new aims. They are opposed to routine which marks an arrest of growth. Since growth is the characteristic of life, education is all one with growing; it has no end beyond itself.

English text, Chapter IV, summary.
