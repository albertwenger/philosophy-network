# Seeing with the whole body and mind

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Seeing and hearing involve the whole body and mind.

## Context and limits

Dōgen contrasts this encounter with mirrorlike reflection. Experiencing one side leaves another unseen, so direct experience is not an exhaustive view.
