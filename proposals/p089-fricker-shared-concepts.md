# Unequal access to shared understanding

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Missing shared concepts can disadvantage marginalized people.

## Context and limits

Fricker links difficulty understanding experience to unequal participation in making social meanings. Her case concerns structural exclusion, not every misunderstanding.

## Source and attribution

[Miranda Fricker](../people/fricker.md); [Epistemic Injustice: Power and the Ethics of Knowing](../sources/fricker-epistemic-injustice.md).
