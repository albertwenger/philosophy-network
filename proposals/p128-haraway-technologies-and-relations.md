# Technologies embody and enforce social relations

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Technologies are shaped by social relations and can help reproduce or change those relations.

## Context and limits

Communication technologies and biotechnologies organize bodies and meanings within historically structured relations.

## Source and attribution

[Donna Haraway](../people/haraway.md); [A Cyborg Manifesto](../sources/haraway-cyborg-manifesto.md).

Passage: The Informatics of Domination, paragraphs on communications technologies, biotechnologies, and the social relations of science.
