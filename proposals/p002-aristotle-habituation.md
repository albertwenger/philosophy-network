# Character through practice

## Core

Repeated actions shape the character from which later actions arise.

## Qualification

Habituation can develop good or bad character; natural capacity alone does not determine the acquired disposition.

## Source and attribution

[Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md); [Nicomachean Ethics, Book II](../sources/aristotle-ethics-ii.md).

Passage: II.1, especially the analogy with learning crafts (1103a–1103b).

This is an editorial reconstruction for evaluation. It is not a quotation or a verified statement of the author’s complete position.
