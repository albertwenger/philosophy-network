# Progress can conceal its victims

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

A civilizing narrative can excuse violence by calling its victims’ suffering a necessary cost of progress.

## Context and limits

Dussel criticizes this justification while retaining a role for emancipatory reason.

## Source and attribution

[Enrique Dussel](../people/dussel.md); [Europe, Modernity, and Eurocentrism](../sources/dussel-europe-modernity.md).

Passage: Printed pages 472–474, numbered account of the myth of modernity and its proposed overcoming.
