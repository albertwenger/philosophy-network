# Habit carries expectation beyond experience

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Repeated experience produces expectations through habit.

## Context and limits

Familiar event sequences prompt belief about unobserved cases. Naming custom explains the transition without proving a hidden causal power or demonstrating future conformity.

## Source excerpt

> Custom, then, is the great guide of human life.

David Hume; Section V, paragraph 36.
