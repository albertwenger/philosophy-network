# Learning and thought need each other

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Learning needs reflection, and reflection needs learning.

## Context and limits

The two warnings constrain each other: accumulating instruction without thought wastes effort, while thought without learning risks going astray. The brief saying does not specify a complete method of inquiry or reject learning from inherited teachings.

## Source excerpt

> The Master said, 'Learning without thought is labour lost; thought without learning is perilous.'

James Legge translation, Book II, chapter XV.
