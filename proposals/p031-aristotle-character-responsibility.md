# Responsibility for becoming a certain person

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Repeated voluntary actions can make us responsible for a character that is no longer easy to change.

## Context and limits

The claim concerns responsibility for beginnings as well as present control. [Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md) distinguishes voluntary actions from slowly acquired states and admits that their development may escape notice. His argument does not say that every condition is chosen: compulsion, relevant ignorance, and conditions not in our power matter.

## Source excerpt

> But actions and states of character are not voluntary in the same way; for we are masters of our actions from the beginning right to the end, if we know the particular facts, but though we control the beginning of our states of character the gradual progress is not obvious any more than it is in illnesses; because it was in our power, however, to act in this way or not in this way, therefore the states are voluntary.

W. D. Ross translation, III.5, 1114b30–1115a3.
