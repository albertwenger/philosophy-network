# Combining what different senses disclose

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

An internal capacity combines information that no single sense receives alone.

## Context and limits

Avicenna uses recognition of honey to distinguish the shared sensory capacity from the individual senses. Imagination and memory have further roles. This is a historical faculty account, not a claim that its accompanying ventricular anatomy is established neuroscience.

## Source excerpt

> the discriminator between two things is he who has known them both

Edward Abbott van Dyck translation; VII.I, p. 61.
