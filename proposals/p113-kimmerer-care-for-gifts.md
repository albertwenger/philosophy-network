# Receiving a gift calls for continued care

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Treating nature’s gifts as relationships can motivate care for their living sources.

## Context and limits

Reciprocity can mean tending plants or protecting habitat, rather than exchanging equally priced objects.
