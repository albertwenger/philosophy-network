# Consciousness named by its conditions

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Sensory consciousness arises with its conditions rather than traveling unchanged.

## Context and limits

The discourse challenges Sāti’s claim that the same consciousness wanders on. It distinguishes awareness dependent on the eye and forms, the other senses, and the intellect and ideas. The interpretation concerns this conditioned consciousness; it does not settle other Buddhist uses of the term.

## Source and attribution

[Discourses attributed to the Buddha](../people/buddha.md); [The Greater Craving-Destruction Discourse](../sources/buddhist-consciousness.md).

Passage: MN 38, rejection of Sāti’s view and “Consciousness Classified by Requisite Condition.”
