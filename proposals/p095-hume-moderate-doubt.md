# Doubt can discipline ordinary inquiry

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Limited skepticism can temper dogmatism without stopping inquiry.

## Context and limits

Hume contrasts enduring caution with total suspension that ordinary activity defeats. He recommends proportioning inquiry to human capacities.

## Source excerpt

> Nature is always too strong for principle.

David Hume; Section XII, paragraph 128, on excessive skepticism.
