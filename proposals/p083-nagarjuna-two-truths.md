# Ordinary conventions and ultimate understanding

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Ultimate teaching depends on ordinary conventions.

## Context and limits

The two truths are distinguished without allowing conventional discourse simply to be discarded. The passage links their understanding to liberation.

## Source and attribution

[Nāgārjuna](../people/nagarjuna.md); [Mūlamadhyamakakārikā](../sources/nagarjuna-middle-verses.md).

Passage: Chapter XXIV, verses 8–10, read after the reply in verse 7; Batchelor column.
