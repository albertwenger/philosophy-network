# Prejudice can wrong a speaker as a knower

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Identity prejudice can unfairly reduce a speaker’s credibility.

## Context and limits

The wrong concerns someone’s participation in giving knowledge. Fricker distinguishes prejudicial dismissal from disbelief supported by reasons.

## Source and attribution

[Miranda Fricker](../people/fricker.md); [Epistemic Injustice: Power and the Ethics of Knowing](../sources/fricker-epistemic-injustice.md).

Passage: Introduction, pp. 1 and 4–5: testimonial injustice and the giver of knowledge.
