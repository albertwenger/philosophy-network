# Time cannot stand independently

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Time’s divisions cannot be established independently of their relations.

## Context and limits

The verses test dependence among past, present, and future, then time’s relation to things. This challenges self-standing time; it is not a measurement theory.
