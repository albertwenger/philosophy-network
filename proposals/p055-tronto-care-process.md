# Care includes responsibility and response

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Meeting needs requires responsibility, work, and attention to results.

## Context and limits

[Tronto](../people/tronto.md)’s phases distinguish recognizing a need from ensuring its provision and evaluating the response.

## Source and attribution

[Joan Tronto](../people/tronto.md); [Caring Democracy](../sources/tronto-caring-democracy.md).

Passage: Chapter 1, “The Meaning of Care and Caring”, numbered phases 1–4.
