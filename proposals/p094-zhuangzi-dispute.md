# Winning a dispute does not settle correctness

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Defeating an opponent does not by itself show whose assertion is correct.

## Context and limits

The speaker questions victory and the choice of an agreeing or disagreeing arbiter. This destabilizes confidence in a dispute’s outcome without establishing that contradictory assertions are equally true. The passage belongs to a literary dialogue, not an independently recorded declaration by the historical author.

## Source excerpt

> Is the one of us right and the other wrong? are we both right or both wrong?

James Legge translation; Book II.10, PDF p. 126.
