# Philosophy through descriptions of language

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

Philosophical clarification rearranges familiar uses of language.

## Context and limits

Section 109 treats the problems at issue as conceptual confusions, not empirical hypotheses. Description is directed by the particular difficulty.

## Source and attribution

[Ludwig Wittgenstein](../people/wittgenstein.md); [Philosophische Untersuchungen](../sources/wittgenstein-investigations.md).

Passage: Part I, §109, read with §§107–110.
