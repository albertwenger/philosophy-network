# Withholding assent for the sake of certainty

The short version and commentary are draft interpretations, not quotations.

## Short version

The search for secure foundations begins by withholding assent wherever doubt remains.

## Context and limits

The meditator need not prove all inherited beliefs false. Finding a reason for doubt is enough to suspend assent during this inquiry. Doubt serves a search for certainty; the opening meditation does not establish that ordinary experience is universally false.

[René Descartes](../people/descartes.md); [Meditations on First Philosophy](../sources/descartes-meditations.md).
