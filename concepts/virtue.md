# Virtue

Virtue is considered through good choice, governing character, helping others develop, moral cultivation, and a standard shared by women and men.

## Character directed toward good choice ([Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md))

A settled disposition to choose well, developed through practice and guided by reason and practical wisdom.

Related proposals:

- [Virtue and the mean](../proposals/p001-aristotle-mean.md)
- [Character through practice](../proposals/p002-aristotle-habituation.md)
- [Responsibility for becoming a certain person](../proposals/p031-aristotle-character-responsibility.md)

## Moral character expressed in governing ([Confucius](../people/confucius.md))

A ruler’s moral character, expressed in leading by example and joined with ritual in governing.

Related proposals:

- [Cultivating character through government](../proposals/p024-confucius-governance.md)

## Moral excellence shared across sexes ([Wollstonecraft](../people/wollstonecraft.md))

Moral excellence judged by the same standard for women and men. The selected passage still allows differences in degree between the sexes.

Related proposals:

- [One moral standard across sexes](../proposals/p027-wollstonecraft-virtue.md)

## Character that helps others develop ([Confucius](../people/confucius.md))

Moral character expressed in helping others establish and develop themselves, guided by comparison with one’s own aspirations.

Related proposals:

- [Helping others to develop](../proposals/p035-confucius-helping-others.md)

## Developed moral beginnings ([Mencius](../people/mencius.md))

Benevolence, righteousness, propriety, and discernment developed from beginnings already present in human beings.

Related proposals:

- [Compassion as a moral beginning](../proposals/p044-mencius-compassion.md)
