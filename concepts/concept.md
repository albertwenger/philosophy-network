# Concept

Must everything in a category have one feature in common?

The further passages examine ideas derived from experience and shared concepts used to understand social life.

## A category held together by similarities ([Wittgenstein](../people/wittgenstein.md))

A category whose members share overlapping similarities, without requiring one feature common to all of them.

Related proposals:

- [Overlapping resemblance](../proposals/p015-wittgenstein-family.md)

## Ideas derived from impressions ([Hume](../people/hume.md))

Mental contents whose simple components derive from impressions, though imagination recombines them.

Related proposals:

- [Ideas and their experiential materials](../proposals/p072-hume-impressions-ideas.md)

## A shared tool for understanding experience ([Fricker](../people/fricker.md))

A shared interpretive resource whose absence can disadvantage marginalized people.

Related proposals:

- [Unequal access to shared understanding](../proposals/p089-fricker-shared-concepts.md)
