# Experience

How do familiarity with particular situations and attention to consequences inform learning and judgment?

## Doing and undergoing connected through consequences ([Dewey](../people/dewey.md))

The connection between what we do and what happens to us as a result, which can guide later activity.

## Familiarity with particulars gained over time ([Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md))

Familiarity with particular situations acquired over time, enabling practical judgment to apply general understanding.
