# Responsibility

Responsibility concerns both answerability for conduct and tasks one should undertake for others.

## Answerability for voluntary character formation ([Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md))

Being answerable for a character one helped form through voluntary actions, even when changing it has become difficult.

Related proposals:

- [Responsibility for becoming a certain person](../proposals/p031-aristotle-character-responsibility.md)

## A duty to help others ([Kant](../people/kant.md))

A requirement to make others’ welfare an end rather than universally refuse assistance.

Related proposals:

- [Why universal indifference cannot be willed](../proposals/p038-kant-mutual-aid.md)

## Duties toward others in shared life ([Gyekye](../people/gyekye.md))

Tasks owed to others through shared humanity.

Related proposals:

- [Shared humanity creates duties](../proposals/p054-gyekye-duties.md)

## Taking charge of meeting a need ([Tronto](../people/tronto.md))

Undertaking to ensure that an identified need is addressed.

Related proposals:

- [Care includes responsibility and response](../proposals/p055-tronto-care-process.md)
