# Obligation

The passages explore moral requirements through argument, universal law, rights, and shared humanity.

## What ought to be done ([Hume](../people/hume.md))

What someone ought to do. An argument must explain how it moves from descriptions to a claim about such a requirement.

Related proposals:

- [From descriptions to what ought to be done](../proposals/p009-hume-is-ought.md)

## A requirement of universal law ([Kant](../people/kant.md))

A moral requirement to act only on principles one can coherently will as laws for everyone.

Related proposals:

- [Universalizing a principle of action](../proposals/p010-kant-universal-law.md)
- [The self-defeat of false promising](../proposals/p037-kant-false-promises.md)
- [Why universal indifference cannot be willed](../proposals/p038-kant-mutual-aid.md)


## Duties inseparable from rights ([Wollstonecraft](../people/wollstonecraft.md))

Moral demands made on rational agents, which cannot consistently be retained while their rights are denied.

Related proposals:

- [Rights and duties stand together](../proposals/p042-wollstonecraft-rights-duties.md)

## Requirements grounded in shared humanity ([Gyekye](../people/gyekye.md))

Duties including aid in distress, extending beyond negotiated exchange.

Related proposals:

- [Shared humanity creates duties](../proposals/p054-gyekye-duties.md)
