# Human nature

Do failures of conduct show what people are by nature, or what has happened to their capacities?

## A tendency toward goodness that can be damaged ([Mencius](../people/mencius.md))

A shared tendency toward goodness whose development depends on cultivation and can be obstructed by circumstances or conduct.

## Apparent nature shaped by education ([Wollstonecraft](../people/wollstonecraft.md))

Traits presented as women’s nature that may instead arise from an education that weakens understanding and encourages dependence.
