# Being

These passages examine existence through divine necessity and through the relation between existing and time.

## Existence identical with divine essence ([Maimonides](../people/maimonides.md))

God’s uncaused existence, distinguished from the caused existence of other beings.

Related proposals:

- [Existence and divine essence](../proposals/p080-maimonides-existence.md)

## Existing as time ([Dōgen](../people/dogen.md))

The temporal existence of concrete things, rather than timeless being.

Related proposals:

- [Existing and time](../proposals/p085-dogen-being-time.md)
