# Personhood

What makes someone a person, and how does this relate to rational agency and social life?

## A rational being capable of moral legislation ([Kant](../people/kant.md))

A rational being able to give universal moral laws and be bound by them, rather than merely serve another’s purposes.

Related proposals:

- [Dignity admits no equivalent](../proposals/p039-kant-dignity.md)

## Social membership with individual capacities ([Gyekye](../people/gyekye.md))

A socially situated person whose capacities are not wholly created by the community.
