# Beauty

Beauty can join form with life, shape ordinary surroundings, or belong to an environment held in common.

## Living form ([Schiller](../people/schiller.md))

Beauty in which sensuous life and intelligible form engage together. Neither bare sensation nor abstract form alone completes it.

Related proposals:

- [Beauty as living form](../proposals/p101-schiller-living-form.md)

## Beauty in ordinary surroundings ([Morris](../people/morris.md))

The beauty of everyday things and shared surroundings, valued for their actual enjoyment rather than their display of wealth.
