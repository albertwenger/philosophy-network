# Pleasure

Pleasure can enter happiness, mark a change in our power to act, or arise within work itself.

## Pleasure as part of happiness ([Mill](../people/mill.md))

Enjoyment, which together with the absence of pain makes up happiness. Pleasures can differ in quality as well as quantity.

Related proposals:

- [The moral criterion of happiness](../proposals/p012-mill-happiness.md)
- [Differences in the quality of pleasure](../proposals/p013-mill-pleasure-quality.md)

- [Happiness need not be constant excitement](../proposals/p118-mill-tranquillity-and-excitement.md)

## A transition toward greater power ([Spinoza](../people/spinoza.md))

A transition to greater perfection or power of acting, distinguished from possessing that condition already.

Related proposals:

- [Pleasure as a change in power](../proposals/p105-spinoza-pleasure-as-transition.md)

## Enjoyment within the work itself ([Morris](../people/morris.md))

Enjoyment consciously experienced while working, rather than only a reward or release after work.

Related proposals:

- [What makes work worth doing](../proposals/p109-morris-worthy-work.md)
