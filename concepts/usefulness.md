# Usefulness

Usefulness depends on the purposes and forms of life through which something’s value is assessed.

## Usefulness for an assumed purpose ([Zhuangzi](../people/zhuangzi.md))

Fitness for a particular practical purpose, such as a tree’s suitability for timber.

Related proposals:

- [The usefulness of an unusable tree](../proposals/p025-zhuangzi-usefulness.md)

## Value beyond the assumed purpose ([Zhuangzi](../people/zhuangzi.md))

The value of a tree’s survival and the ease it offers when it is no longer judged as timber.

Related proposals:

- [The usefulness of an unusable tree](../proposals/p025-zhuangzi-usefulness.md)

## What supports rational preservation of life ([Spinoza](../people/spinoza.md))

What reason recognizes as supporting our being and activity. Seeking it can involve wanting the same good for others.

Related proposals:

- [Rational advantage can be shared](../proposals/p106-spinoza-common-usefulness.md)

## A product worth making and having ([Morris](../people/morris.md))

The contribution of a product to a worthwhile life, assessed together with the cost and character of the work that makes it.

Related proposals:

- [What makes work worth doing](../proposals/p109-morris-worthy-work.md)
