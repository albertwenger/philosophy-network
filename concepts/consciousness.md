# Consciousness

The passages examine present thinking, felt sensations, and awareness arising under particular conditions.

## Present awareness in thinking ([Descartes](../people/descartes.md))

Present thinking, including doubting, willing, imagining, and seeming to sense.

Related proposals:

- [What the thinking subject does](../proposals/p070-descartes-modes-thinking.md)

## Having a sensation ([Wittgenstein](../people/wittgenstein.md))

Felt experience such as pain, distinguished from knowing or describing it.

Related proposals:

- [Learning to express pain](../proposals/p077-wittgenstein-pain.md)

## Awareness dependent on conditions ([Buddha](../people/buddha.md))

Awareness classified by conditions such as the eye and visible forms, rather than an unchanged traveler.

Related proposals:

- [Consciousness named by its conditions](../proposals/p093-buddhist-conditioned-consciousness.md)
