# Reason

The passages consider reasoning as a source of judgment, a guide to action, and a practice affected by social power.

## Discovering relations and means ([Hume](../people/hume.md))

Reasoning that identifies truths and means but, on this account, supplies no motive by itself.

Related proposals:

- [Reason can direct a motive without supplying it](../proposals/p075-hume-reason-motive.md)

## Reasons distinguished from prejudice ([Fricker](../people/fricker.md))

Grounds for accepting or rejecting claims, distinguishable from social pressure.

Related proposals:

- [Reason can be situated without becoming mere power](../proposals/p090-fricker-reason-power.md)

## The acquisition of intelligible forms ([Avicenna](../people/avicenna.md))

Intellectual activity that develops through basic principles, demonstration, and comparison of sensory materials.

Related proposals:

- [How intellectual understanding develops](../proposals/p092-avicenna-intellect.md)
