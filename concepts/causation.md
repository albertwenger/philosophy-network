# Causation

These passages examine repeated sequences, the felt necessity of a connection, and the conditional arising of things without independent nature.

## Felt necessity ([Hume](../people/hume.md))

The felt connection that leads one to expect an event when another occurs, formed through repeated experience.

Related proposals:

- [Experienced regularity and felt necessity](../proposals/p008-hume-necessary-connection.md)

## Repeated sequences of events ([Hume](../people/hume.md))

The repeated experience of one kind of event followed by another.

Related proposals:

- [Experienced regularity and felt necessity](../proposals/p008-hume-necessary-connection.md)
- [Habit carries expectation beyond experience](../proposals/p073-hume-custom-expectation.md)

## Arising through dependence ([Nāgārjuna](../people/nagarjuna.md))

The conditional arising of things, connected with their lack of independent nature.

Related proposals:

- [Dependence and emptiness](../proposals/p084-nagarjuna-dependent-things.md)
