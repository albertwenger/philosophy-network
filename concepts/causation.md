# Causation

The contrast is between events repeatedly following one another and the felt necessity of their connection.

## Felt necessity ([Hume](../people/hume.md))

The felt connection that leads one to expect an event when another occurs, formed through repeated experience.

- [Experienced regularity and felt necessity](../proposals/p008-hume-necessary-connection.md)

## Repeated sequences of events ([Hume](../people/hume.md))

The repeated experience of one kind of event followed by another.
