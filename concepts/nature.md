# Nature

Nature can mean a common causal order, a community of living relations, or a boundary whose supposed purity is questioned.

## The common order of natural causes ([Spinoza](../people/spinoza.md))

The causal order to which human actions and emotions belong, alongside everything else. Human passions are not exceptions to nature’s laws.

Related proposals:

- [Understanding emotions as natural events](../proposals/p103-spinoza-emotions-in-nature.md)

## A living community of gift relations ([Kimmerer](../people/kimmerer.md))

Plants and other beings encountered as relatives whose gifts call for care.

Related proposals:

- [Receiving a gift calls for continued care](../proposals/p113-kimmerer-care-for-gifts.md)

## A contested division between organisms and machines ([Haraway](../people/haraway.md))

The natural understood through boundaries whose stability technologies and political practices disturb.

Related proposals:

- [The cyborg unsettles natural boundaries](../proposals/p117-haraway-cyborg-boundaries.md)

## Living systems as teachers of limits ([Kimmerer](../people/kimmerer.md))

Moss communities considered as examples from which humans can learn about ecological limits.

Related proposals:

- [Learning persistence from moss communities](../proposals/p126-kimmerer-learning-from-moss.md)
