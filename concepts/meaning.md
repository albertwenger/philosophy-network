# Meaning

How can looking at a word’s use help explain its meaning?

Other passages examine negative descriptions of God and understanding expressed through rule-governed practice.

## A word’s use ([Wittgenstein](../people/wittgenstein.md))

For many cases, a word’s meaning can be explained by its use. This is a limited claim, not a definition of all meaning.

## Understanding expressed in practice ([Wittgenstein](../people/wittgenstein.md))

Understanding shown in applying rules, rather than in further interpretations alone.

## Divine descriptions understood negatively ([Maimonides](../people/maimonides.md))

Descriptions of God that exclude defects rather than name qualities added to an essence.
