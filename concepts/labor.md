# Labor

Work can make useful things and offer pleasure, while the conditions of production can separate workers from their activity and its results.

## Productive activity under estranging conditions ([Marx](../people/marx.md))

Work that produces objects while its activity and results can be controlled by others.

Related proposals:

- [When a product gains power over its maker](../proposals/p107-marx-alienated-product.md)
- [Estrangement within the act of working](../proposals/p108-marx-alienated-activity.md)

## Work made worth doing ([Morris](../people/morris.md))

Activity worth undertaking through its useful product, the pleasure of doing it, and sufficient rest.
