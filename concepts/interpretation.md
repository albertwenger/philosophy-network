# Interpretation

Interpreting can reconstruct a history, make knowledge answerable to a situated perspective, or guide a reader’s conduct.

## Reframing a history through its excluded relations ([Dussel](../people/dussel.md))

Reconstructing modernity through colonial relations omitted from a story centered within Europe.

Related proposals:

- [Reading modernity through colonial relations](../proposals/p114-dussel-reframing-modernity.md)

## Accountable interpretation from a particular position ([Haraway](../people/haraway.md))

Ongoing critical interpretation that acknowledges its location and remains answerable to other perspectives.

Related proposals:

- [Interpretation needs an accountable position](../proposals/p116-haraway-accountable-interpretation.md)

## Understanding a text in order to live by it ([Epictetus](../people/epictetus.md))

Clarifying a philosophical text as a step toward acting in accordance with its teaching, rather than displaying scholarly skill alone.

Related proposals:

- [Interpreting philosophy should lead into practice](../proposals/p123-epictetus-interpretation-and-practice.md)
