# Emotion

Are emotions natural processes, changes in our power to act, or responses shaped by judgments?

## Changes in the power to act ([Spinoza](../people/spinoza.md))

Bodily changes and their ideas that increase or diminish the power to act. Their causes can be investigated rather than merely condemned.

Related proposals:

- [Understanding emotions as natural events](../proposals/p103-spinoza-emotions-in-nature.md)
- [Pleasure as a change in power](../proposals/p105-spinoza-pleasure-as-transition.md)

## Provocation shaped by an opinion ([Epictetus](../people/epictetus.md))

Feeling affronted through the opinion one forms of another’s words or actions; a pause can make room to examine that opinion.
