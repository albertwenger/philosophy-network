# Art

Art appears here as the animation of material form and as a way of making daily life worth attending to.

## Giving material a living form ([Schiller](../people/schiller.md))

The shaping of material so that form becomes alive in feeling and life becomes intelligible. A sculpted stone can have this quality without being biologically alive.

Related proposals:

- [Beauty as living form](../proposals/p101-schiller-living-form.md)

## Making daily life a source of interest ([Morris](../people/morris.md))

The thoughtful shaping of ordinary objects and activities so that daily life offers interest and enjoyment.
