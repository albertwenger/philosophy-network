# Belief

What is involved in accepting something as so? These senses concern assent and expectation; they overlap with the uses of opinion explored on the [Opinion](opinion.md) page.

## Expectation produced by habit ([Hume](../people/hume.md))

Assent to an unobserved event prompted by familiar experience rather than demonstration.

Related proposals:

- [Habit carries expectation beyond experience](../proposals/p073-hume-custom-expectation.md)

## Conviction joined to understanding ([Maimonides](../people/maimonides.md))

Accepting an understood claim as corresponding to reality, rather than merely saying its words.

Related proposals:

- [Belief requires understanding](../proposals/p079-maimonides-belief.md)
