# Reality

What distinguishes appearances from what is real, and can things be real without existing independently?

## What appearances participate in ([Plato](../people/plato.md))

Beauty itself and other Forms, distinguished from the many things that participate in them.

## Things without independent nature ([Nāgārjuna](../people/nagarjuna.md))
