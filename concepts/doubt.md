# Doubt

Doubt can suspend assent, restrain confidence, or question whether a dispute has established its conclusion.

## Suspending assent to what is doubtful ([Descartes](../people/descartes.md))

Withholding assent when a reason for doubt remains, as a method of seeking secure foundations.

Related proposals:

- [Withholding assent for the sake of certainty](../proposals/p068-descartes-withholding-assent.md)

## Caution within ordinary inquiry ([Hume](../people/hume.md))

Limited skepticism that moderates confidence while allowing ordinary inquiry and action.

Related proposals:

- [Doubt can discipline ordinary inquiry](../proposals/p095-hume-moderate-doubt.md)

## Questioning victory in argument ([Zhuangzi](../people/zhuangzi.md))

Uncertainty about whether defeating an opponent or choosing an arbiter establishes correctness.

Related proposals:

- [Winning a dispute does not settle correctness](../proposals/p094-zhuangzi-dispute.md)
