# Habit

How do repeated actions shape character and a capacity to act?

## Character formed by repeated actions ([Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md))

A settled disposition formed through repeated actions, which shapes later conduct and may become difficult to change at will.

Related proposals:

- [Responsibility for becoming a certain person](../proposals/p031-aristotle-character-responsibility.md)
- [Character through practice](../proposals/p002-aristotle-habituation.md)

## An acquired capacity for effective action ([Dewey](../people/dewey.md))

A disposition acquired through experience that enables action within an environment; it can support growth or harden into routine.
