# Power

The passages distinguish governing oneself, exercising political influence, and directing another’s work.

## Government of oneself through reason ([Wollstonecraft](../people/wollstonecraft.md))

The capacity to direct one’s own conduct through cultivated reason, contrasted with influence gained by pleasing men.

Related proposals:

- [Power to govern oneself](../proposals/p041-wollstonecraft-self-government.md)

## Political influence exercised through the vote ([Du Bois](../people/du-bois.md))

The ability to defend civic standing through political participation, threatened by disenfranchisement and racial domination.

Related proposals:

- [Civic rights and the demand for respect](../proposals/p043-du-bois-civic-standing.md)

## Control within a care relationship ([Tronto](../people/tronto.md))

Who directs care work; recipients can hold greater power than workers.

Related proposals:

- [Power can run toward the recipient](../proposals/p057-tronto-care-power.md)
