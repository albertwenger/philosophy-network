# End

The passages distinguish beings valued in themselves from purposes that guide education and action.

## An end in itself ([Kant](../people/kant.md))

A rational being whose value does not depend on serving someone’s purposes, and who must never be treated merely as an instrument.

Related proposals:

- [Humanity as an end](../proposals/p011-kant-humanity.md)
- [Dignity admits no equivalent](../proposals/p039-kant-dignity.md)
- [A community of ends and lawmakers](../proposals/p040-kant-kingdom-ends.md)

## The purpose of education ([Du Bois](../people/du-bois.md))

The development of whole people and Black communities beyond their earning capacity.

Related proposals:

- [Education beyond earning a living](../proposals/p030-du-bois-education.md)

## An aim guiding an ongoing activity ([Dewey](../people/dewey.md))

An anticipated outcome that guides observation, choice, and the organization of a present activity, while remaining open to revision.

Related proposals:

- [Educational aims belong within learning](../proposals/p051-dewey-aims.md)
