# Community

How can people share laws, interests, and social practices while retaining their individual standing?

## An ideal union under common moral laws ([Kant](../people/kant.md))

A possible union of rational beings who treat one another as ends under laws they can all give and obey.

Related proposals:

- [A community of ends and lawmakers](../proposals/p040-kant-kingdom-ends.md)

## Associated life through shared interests ([Dewey](../people/dewey.md))

People participating in interests and activities whose meanings are communicated and can be revised through contact with others.

Related proposals:

- [Democracy as shared and open association](../proposals/p050-dewey-democratic-association.md)

## Shared life with individual standing ([Gyekye](../people/gyekye.md))

Shared values and welfare compatible with individual judgment and rights.

Related proposals:

- [Persons exceed their social formation](../proposals/p052-gyekye-social-person.md)
- [Shared humanity creates duties](../proposals/p054-gyekye-duties.md)
