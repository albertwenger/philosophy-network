# Equality

Equality is considered through moral standards, proportions in distribution, correction of injury, civic standing, and democratic care.

## A shared moral standard ([Wollstonecraft](../people/wollstonecraft.md))

The same standard of moral virtue for women and men. The selected passage on virtue still allows differences in degree between the sexes.

Related proposals:

- [One moral standard across sexes](../proposals/p027-wollstonecraft-virtue.md)

## Equal ratios in distribution ([Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md))

Equality between the ratio of people’s relevant standing and the ratio of the shares they receive.

Related proposals:

- [Justice in distributing shared goods](../proposals/p032-aristotle-distribution.md)

## Equal standing in the correction of injury ([Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md))

Treating parties as equals when rectifying injury, rather than weighing their relative virtue.

Related proposals:

- [Correcting injury between equals](../proposals/p033-aristotle-correction.md)

## Rights shared by women and men ([Wollstonecraft](../people/wollstonecraft.md))

Women’s claim to share the rights that accompany men’s standing as rational moral agents.

Related proposals:

- [Rights and duties stand together](../proposals/p042-wollstonecraft-rights-duties.md)

## Civic equality across the color line ([Du Bois](../people/du-bois.md))

Equal civic standing for Black Americans, with voting restrictions judged by the same racial standard.

Related proposals:

- [Civic rights and the demand for respect](../proposals/p043-du-bois-civic-standing.md)

## Equal standing in democratic care ([Tronto](../people/tronto.md))

A democratic commitment governing how caring needs are met.

Related proposals:

- [Care must answer to democratic commitments](../proposals/p056-tronto-democratic-care.md)
