# Happiness

A satisfying life may involve varied pleasures, absorbing daily activity, and ends that become part of happiness itself.

## A satisfying life with varied constituents ([Mill](../people/mill.md))

A life combining enjoyment and freedom from pain, with room for tranquillity, excitement, and attachments that become valuable in themselves.

Related proposals:

- [Happiness need not be constant excitement](../proposals/p118-mill-tranquillity-and-excitement.md)
- [An acquired end can become part of happiness](../proposals/p127-mill-virtue-as-part-of-happiness.md)

## Engagement with the details of daily life ([Morris](../people/morris.md))

A life in which ordinary activity attracts genuine interest instead of being passed off as drudgery for others.

Related proposals:

- [Happiness in the details of ordinary life](../proposals/p125-morris-interest-in-daily-life.md)
