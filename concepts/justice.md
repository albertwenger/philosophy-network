# Justice

These passages distinguish distribution, correction of injury, equitable judgment, and democratic commitments.

## Proportionate distribution ([Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md))

Distributing shared goods in proportion to the relevant standing or merit of recipients. What counts as merit is disputed.

Related proposals:

- [Justice in distributing shared goods](../proposals/p032-aristotle-distribution.md)

## Correction of unequal gains and losses ([Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md))

A judge’s restoration of equality after an injury or wrongful transaction, independently of the parties’ relative moral worth.

Related proposals:

- [Correcting injury between equals](../proposals/p033-aristotle-correction.md)

## Correction of an overgeneral law ([Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md))

Equity corrects a general legal rule when an exceptional case would otherwise defeat the justice the legislator intended.

Related proposals:

- [Equity corrects an overgeneral rule](../proposals/p034-aristotle-equity.md)

## A democratic requirement for care ([Tronto](../people/tronto.md))

A standard that care arrangements must meet alongside equality and freedom.

Related proposals:

- [Care must answer to democratic commitments](../proposals/p056-tronto-democratic-care.md)
