# Embodiment

How does experience involve a body: through its union with mind, sensory organs, or bodily practice?

## The experienced union of mind and body ([Descartes](../people/descartes.md))

Mind and body experienced as one whole through sensations such as pain and hunger.

Related proposals:

- [Pain and the union of mind and body](../proposals/p071-descartes-body-union.md)

## Experiencing with the whole body and mind ([Dōgen](../people/dogen.md))

Seeing and hearing as participation of the whole body and mind.

Related proposals:

- [Seeing with the whole body and mind](../proposals/p087-dogen-embodied-seeing.md)

## Bodily conditions of sensory awareness ([Buddha](../people/buddha.md))

The sense organs’ role in the arising of sensory awareness; mental awareness is also distinguished.

Related proposals:

- [Consciousness named by its conditions](../proposals/p093-buddhist-conditioned-consciousness.md)
