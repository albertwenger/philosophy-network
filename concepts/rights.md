# Rights

What claims should people have, and how do these connect to duties and social membership?

## Claims belonging to rational moral agents ([Wollstonecraft](../people/wollstonecraft.md))

Claims to freedom and civil standing that accompany women’s capacity for reason and moral duty.

Related proposals:

- [Rights and duties stand together](../proposals/p042-wollstonecraft-rights-duties.md)

## Voting and civic standing ([Du Bois](../people/du-bois.md))

Claims to the vote, civic equality, and education, asserted against racial exclusion.

Related proposals:

- [Civic rights and the demand for respect](../proposals/p043-du-bois-civic-standing.md)

## Individual claims within communal life ([Gyekye](../people/gyekye.md))

Individual claims acknowledged within community, without necessarily overriding every common good.

Related proposals:

- [Dignity supports rights within community](../proposals/p053-gyekye-dignity-rights.md)
