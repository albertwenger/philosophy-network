# Education

The passages consider how education shapes character, joins learning with thought, develops understanding, and sustains further growth.

## Character shaped through practice ([Aristotle](../people/aristotle.md))

The shaping of character through repeated actions. This process can develop good or bad character.

Related proposals:

- [Character through practice](../proposals/p002-aristotle-habituation.md)

## Character shaped through example and ritual ([Confucius](../people/confucius.md))

The shaping of moral character through a ruler’s example and ritual practice.

Related proposals:

- [Cultivating character through government](../proposals/p024-confucius-governance.md)

## The development of understanding ([Wollstonecraft](../people/wollstonecraft.md))

The development of understanding, contrasted with training women to be attractive and dependent.

Related proposals:

- [Education and apparent nature](../proposals/p028-wollstonecraft-education.md)
- [Power to govern oneself](../proposals/p041-wollstonecraft-self-government.md)

## The development of people and communities ([Du Bois](../people/du-bois.md))

Education directed toward the development of whole people and Black communities, including intellectual and vocational training.

Related proposals:

- [Education beyond earning a living](../proposals/p030-du-bois-education.md)

## Learning joined with thought ([Confucius](../people/confucius.md))

Learning and reflective thought as mutually necessary parts of intellectual development.

Related proposals:

- [Learning and thought need each other](../proposals/p036-confucius-learning-thought.md)

## Nourishment of moral capacities ([Mencius](../people/mencius.md))

Sustaining the conditions in which moral capacities can grow, rather than repeatedly damaging them.

Related proposals:

- [Damaged capacities are not absent capacities](../proposals/p047-mencius-nourishment.md)

## Continuing capacity for growth ([Dewey](../people/dewey.md))

Learning that develops the capacity for further learning through intelligent activity and its consequences.

Related proposals:

- [Habits can keep learning alive](../proposals/p048-dewey-habit.md)
- [Learning from what an action brings about](../proposals/p049-dewey-consequences.md)
- [Educational aims belong within learning](../proposals/p051-dewey-aims.md)
