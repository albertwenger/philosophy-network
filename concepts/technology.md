# Technology

Technologies can reorganize work, carry social relations, and unsettle the division between living beings and machines.

## Machinery organized to reduce burdensome work ([Morris](../people/morris.md))

Machines assessed by how they reduce necessary unattractive labor for the community, rather than simply raise an owner’s profits.

Related proposals:

- [Machinery should lighten common burdens](../proposals/p110-morris-machinery-and-progress.md)

## Tools that embody social relations ([Haraway](../people/haraway.md))

Instruments and systems shaped by social relations that also help enforce meanings and reorganize bodies.

Related proposals:

- [Technologies embody and enforce social relations](../proposals/p128-haraway-technologies-and-relations.md)

## A hybrid relation between organisms and machines ([Haraway](../people/haraway.md))

Technological relations that unsettle the separation between living organisms and artificial machines.

Related proposals:

- [The cyborg unsettles natural boundaries](../proposals/p117-haraway-cyborg-boundaries.md)
