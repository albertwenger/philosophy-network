# Opinion

What can a true opinion do without amounting to knowledge?

The Republic adds a different contrast between opinion about appearances and knowledge of Forms. These uses overlap with, rather than universally exclude, [belief](belief.md).

## True opinion that guides action ([Plato](../people/plato.md))

A true opinion that can guide action successfully without being knowledge.

## Opinion about the many appearances ([Plato](../people/plato.md))

Cognition of particular beautiful things without distinguishing them from beauty itself.
