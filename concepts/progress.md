# Progress

More production or a claim to civilization need not settle whether a change improves human life.

## Improvement measured by the lives of workers ([Morris](../people/morris.md))

A gain in shared leisure and relief from burdensome work. More ingenious or profitable machines do not alone establish such a gain.

Related proposals:

- [Machinery should lighten common burdens](../proposals/p110-morris-machinery-and-progress.md)

## A civilizing narrative under criticism ([Dussel](../people/dussel.md))

A claimed advance that can disguise domination by treating its victims’ suffering as necessary.

Related proposals:

- [Progress can conceal its victims](../proposals/p115-dussel-costs-of-civilizing.md)
