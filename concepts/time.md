# Time

The passages question whether time can be separated from things, events, and their relations.

## Time examined through dependencies ([Nāgārjuna](../people/nagarjuna.md))

Past, present, and future examined as mutually dependent rather than self-standing.

Related proposals:

- [Time cannot stand independently](../proposals/p082-nagarjuna-time.md)

## Time inseparable from existing ([Dōgen](../people/dogen.md))

Time understood through existing things and activity, not as an external container.

Related proposals:

- [Existing and time](../proposals/p085-dogen-being-time.md)
- [Firewood and ash in their own times](../proposals/p086-dogen-firewood-ash.md)
