# Substance

Is a thing a persisting bearer of qualities, a collection of qualities, or something to which existence is added?

## The body understood through changing qualities ([Descartes](../people/descartes.md))

The extended body understood to remain the same through changes in its sensory qualities.

Related proposals:

- [The wax through its alterations](../proposals/p069-descartes-wax.md)

## A collection of qualities ([Hume](../people/hume.md))

Qualities united in imagination; a separate bearer beyond them is challenged.

Related proposals:

- [Substance and its supposed bearer](../proposals/p074-hume-substance-qualities.md)

## A subject bearing additional attributes ([Maimonides](../people/maimonides.md))

A bearer to which existence or other attributes are added; this description is rejected for God.

Related proposals:

- [Existence and divine essence](../proposals/p080-maimonides-existence.md)
