## Text and reuse

The [Wikisource edition notice](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Chinese_Classics/Volume_2/The_Works_of_Mencius) identifies the original and Legge translation as public domain, published before 1931. The excerpts reproduce that transcription. The [1875 edition record and scan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Life_and_works_of_Mencius,_by_James_Legge,_1875,_from_the_Internet_Archive_-_lifeworksofmenci00mencuoft.pdf) independently document an early public-domain Legge edition; it is supporting reuse evidence, not the transcription used here. Jurisdiction: United States. Checked 2026-09-26.

## Passages consulted

Inspected 1A7, 2A6, 6A2, and 6A8 in Legge’s English text, including the king’s ox episode, the child at the well, the exchange with Gaozi, and the Niu Mountain analogy. The chapter pages are [Book I, Part I](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Chinese_Classics/Volume_2/The_Works_of_Mencius/chapter01), [Book II, Part I](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Chinese_Classics/Volume_2/The_Works_of_Mencius/chapter03), and [Book VI, Part I](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Chinese_Classics/Volume_2/The_Works_of_Mencius/chapter11). The cited locators identify the traditional numbered passages; no independent review of the Chinese or the whole work is claimed.
