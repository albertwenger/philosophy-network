## Text and reuse

Carter’s translation was first published in 1758; excerpts use the 1759 edition, which is public domain in the United States. See the [1758 library record](https://catalog.folger.edu/record/752850) and the [1759 edition and rights statement](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/All_the_Works_of_Epictetus,_Which_Are_Now_Extant). Original spelling and capitalization are retained; long s is rendered as s. Checked 2026-09-26.
