## Passages consulted

Part I, chapters L, LVII, and LVIII were inspected for belief, divine existence, and negative attributes, including their theological qualifications. The original Arabic was not independently checked.

The check concerns selected passages, not independent scholarly review of the edition or work.

- [Knowing through what cannot be said of God](../proposals/p081-maimonides-negation.md): Part I, chapter LVIII, opening explanation and its application, pp. 81–83.
