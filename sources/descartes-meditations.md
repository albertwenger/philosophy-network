## Passages consulted

The opening method in Meditation I, thinking and wax in Meditation II, and the body-union passage in Meditation VI were inspected. The inspection did not cover every argument of the work. The full-work link covers the six Meditations, not all later objections and replies.

The check concerns selected passages, not independent scholarly review of the edition or work.
