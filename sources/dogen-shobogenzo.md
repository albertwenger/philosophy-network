Edition: Gudo Wafu Nishijima and Chodo Cross translation, Master Dogen’s Shobogenzo, Windbell Publications; Book 1 copyright 1994, distributed as an ebook by Dogen Sangha.

[Read Book 1 (PDF)](https://dogensanghas.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/shobogenzo-ebook-1.pdf) · [Access the complete four-book edition](https://dogensanghas.com/resources/)

The cited PDF is Book 1 only. The downloads page provides Books 1–4 of this translation.

Contributor: [Dōgen](../people/dogen.md).

## Text and reuse

Book 1’s copyright page (PDF p. 5) reserves rights to Nishijima and Cross. The modern translation is not public domain in the United States; proposals therefore contain no excerpts. Access and notice checked 2026-09-26.

## Passages consulted

Inspected Genjo-koan [85], [87] and Uji [29]–[32], excluding introductions. PDF pages count the cover; Books 2–4 were not inspected.

The check concerns selected passages, not independent scholarly review of the edition or work.
