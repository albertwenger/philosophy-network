## Text and reuse

The publisher makes the PDF available for research, but its copyright page reserves all rights. This edition is not verified as public domain in the United States, so proposals are linked without excerpts. Checked 2026-09-26.

## Passages consulted

Inspected printed pp. 109–119, especially pp. 112–114, 114–117, and 117–119 (PDF pages 118–120, 120–123, and 123–125 when counted from 1). The title printed on the essay is used here; the volume contents instead label it “Person and Community in Akan Thought”. Inspection concerns these passages, not the complete anthology.
