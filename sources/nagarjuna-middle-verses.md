## Text and reuse

## Passages consulted

The Batchelor column was read at XIX.1–6 and XXIV.7–10, 17–20. The adjacent Streng translation and commentarial columns were not substituted for it. Only these verses and their immediate context were inspected.

The check concerns selected passages, not independent scholarly review of the edition or work.
