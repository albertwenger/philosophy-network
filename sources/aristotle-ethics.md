Edition: W. D. Ross translation (1925), Internet Classics Archive.

[Read the full work](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.html) · [Read Book II](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.2.ii.html) · [Read Book VI](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.6.vi.html)

## Text and reuse

W. D. Ross’s 1925 translation is public domain in the United States. Cleveland State University’s [translation note](https://pressbooks.ulib.csuohio.edu/nicomacheanethics/front-matter/note-on-translation/) confirms the publication date and public-domain status of Ross’s original translation. Excerpts follow the Internet Classics Archive transcription. Checked 2026-09-26.

## Passages consulted

II.1 and II.6 consulted. The definition in II.6 was checked again during the language revision for its appeal to reason and practical wisdom.

VI.7–8 consulted for the relation between general understanding, particular situations, and experience.

The check covered these passages and their immediate context. Independent source review is still needed.

Additional inspection: III.5 on responsibility for character; V.3 on distribution, V.4 on rectification, and V.10 on equity. The [Book III text](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.3.iii.html) and [Book V text](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.5.v.html) were checked on 2026-09-26.
