# Case 10: Universalizing a principle of action

[Read the proposal](../../proposals/p010-kant-universal-law.md)

## Expected context need

Expected context: **Context and limits**. Context and limits explicitly distinguish the universal-law test from popularity and may help readers answer this question about unanimous approval.

## Possible misreading

Whatever most people approve is morally permissible.

## Reader question

Would unanimous approval of an exception for one favored person settle whether it passes this test?

## Draft answer guide

No. The test concerns whether the principle of action can be coherently willed as a law for everyone. Approval alone does not settle that question.
