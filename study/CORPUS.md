## Sampling limits

Selection favors accessible, familiar texts and identifiable passages. It covers several traditions and forms but is not representative of philosophy as a whole. Important omissions include contemporary work, sustained phenomenology, medieval Islamic and Jewish philosophy, several Indian philosophical schools, Indigenous and oral traditions, and less canonical material. One writer cannot represent a region, tradition, or social experience.

Later samples should include unfamiliar passages and alternative translations, with independent source review to identify errors that familiarity may conceal.
