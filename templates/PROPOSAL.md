# Proposal template

Copy an existing proposal, assign a new ID, and preserve the standard fields.
Keep one candidate core, its necessary qualification, an exact source locator,
attribution status, and explicit links to concept senses.

Record a competing interpretation as another proposal if it would change a
substantive answer. Identify contemporary generalizations and improvements
as such, with a link to their source of inspiration.

Add a study case with a plausible rival, an application probe, a provisional
reviewer key, and the expected failure from further shortening. Keep actual
reader responses separate from these authoring materials.
