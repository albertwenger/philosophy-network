# Concept template

Copy an existing concept record and give it a new stable ID. For each sense,
record:

- A stable local sense identifier and matching Markdown heading.
- A short editorial gloss.
- The proposal or proposals that articulate, use, or challenge the sense.
- What makes it distinct from a nearby sense.
- Any uncertainty about the boundary or attribution.

Avoid giving the family of senses one authoritative definition by default.
Add an explicit proposed equivalence only when its scope and grounds can be
stated. Every associated proposal should link back to the exact sense.
