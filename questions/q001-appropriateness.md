# What guides appropriate choice?

The [proposal about the mean](../proposals/p001-aristotle-mean.md) identifies
reason and practical wisdom as the standard of appropriate choice. What does
a reader need to recognize and apply that standard in a new situation?

The study should examine whether the linked explanations help readers judge
a case. Naming practical wisdom does not by itself show how to exercise it.

An open editorial question about applying the standard, not a refutation.
